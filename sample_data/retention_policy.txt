Title: Data Retention Policy
Last Reviewed: 2019-06-15

Customer transaction records must be retained for a minimum of seven years for regulatory compliance.
Employee records must be retained for the duration of employment plus five years.
Log files must be retained for at least 90 days to support incident investigation.
Backup archives must not be retained beyond three years unless subject to a legal hold.
Personal data must be deleted upon a valid customer erasure request within 30 days.
Marketing email lists must not retain contact records for customers who have unsubscribed.
Financial audit logs should be reviewed quarterly by the compliance team.
