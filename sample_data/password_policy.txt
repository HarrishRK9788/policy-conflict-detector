Title: Corporate Password Policy
Last Reviewed: 2025-09-12

All user passwords must be rotated every 90 days to reduce the risk of credential compromise.
Passwords must not be shared with any third party, contractor, or colleague under any circumstances.
Employees should use a unique password for every corporate system and must not reuse passwords across services.
All passwords must be encrypted at rest using industry standard algorithms.
Password hashes must not use MD5 for storage due to known collision vulnerabilities.
Service accounts must rotate credentials at least once per year.
Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access to corporate systems.
Users must not write down passwords on physical notes left at their workstation.
